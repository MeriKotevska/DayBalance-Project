Morning Walk

Start the day with fresh air and a gentle walk. It does not need to be fast or far — the point is to give yourself a calm beginning.

Easy
30 minutes
Morning
Every day

Why it helps

  • Builds a quiet transition into the day.
  • Supports regular movement without needing equipment.
  • Can improve focus before work or study.
