Healthy Tips

Practical ideas for building routines that feel supportive, not strict.

Hydration

Keep water within reach

A bottle on your desk makes the helpful choice the easy choice. Take a few sips whenever you change tasks.

Sleep

Give your evening a landing place

Try a short, repeatable wind-down routine instead of waiting until you feel tired enough to stop.

Movement

Start smaller than you think

Five minutes of stretching still counts. A habit that is easy to begin is easier to repeat.

Nutrition

Make breakfast visible

Prepare one simple option the night before so the morning decision is already made.

Mental wellbeing

Leave room between tasks

A few quiet minutes can help your attention reset before you move on to the next thing.

Sleep

Protect your usual bedtime

Regularity matters more than chasing a perfect night. Keep your target realistic and forgiving.
